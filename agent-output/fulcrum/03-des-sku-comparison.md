## Recovery and Supporting Charges

Actual owner inputs bind the displayed data volumes and **thirty-day** recovery together.
There is no seven-day fallback. Cosmos continuous backup is a paid 30-day tier with
10 GB × one region as its documented storage billing basis; restore is separately billed.
Blob PITR is 30 days, supporting soft delete and change feed are at least 31 days.
The adopted 10 GB protected versions/deletions and 0.5 GB change feed remain aggregate
planning storage assumptions, not a retention-derived measurement; retention does not
automatically multiply bytes by a fixed factor. Version lifecycle must not delete data
needed for recovery. Churn, accumulation after lifecycle deletion and actual retained
bytes need later monitoring and cost validation.

The committed estimate must include four steady private endpoints, a temporary Cosmos
restore endpoint/account, ACR Premium, live/protected Blob and its restore operations,
workspace ingestion/alerts, and the complete 80-hour access resources. Shared central
DNS zone/link deployment and allocation remain tenant-unverified: no duplicate local
zone deployment or zero-cost claim is made. Current access transfer/restore-lifetime assumptions and DNS allocation treatment
are settled in the69-input projection and actual receipts; earlier pending wording
is historical. No repeat confirmation is required.

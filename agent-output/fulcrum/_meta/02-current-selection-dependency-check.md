## Current inputs and recovery reconciliation

Six actual responses retain their original local request/owner/DEV provenance.
Access is 80 provisioned hours/month; the original freeform string is preserved
in its actual receipt. App hosting remains 730 hours. Thirty-day Cosmos/Blob
application recovery applies to recoverable logical/deletion/application faults;
the 4-hour RTO and 1-hour RPO are goals, not measured recovery. Permanent regional
loss remains provider-dependent and is not solved by the narrow ACR/Entra exceptions.

Cosmos continuous 30-day backup has its paid storage meter, based on 10 GB live data
and one region, plus a separate monthly 10 GB restore event; the successful comparison
includes both. Blob PITR requires Standard GPv2 block blobs without HNS, versioning,
change feed and soft delete. PITR 30 days means supporting soft delete at least
31 days; change-feed retention and version lifecycle must preserve the recoverable
window. The adopted aggregate 10 GB protected versions/deletions and 0.5 GB change
feed are planning volumes jointly consumed with the retention choice. They are not
seven-day storage measurements, and there is no deterministic retention-to-byte
multiplier. Actual churn/retention accumulation may exceed them and must be monitored.

## Supporting billing boundaries

- Steady host/registry/data/monitoring and four service PEs remain provisioned throughout
  the 730-hour envelope. PE traffic is the exact adopted per-endpoint profile, not zero.
- Ephemeral access means **all** billable VM/disk/Bastion/NAT/IP resources end after
  the bounded sessions; VM deallocation alone does not stop other meters. This is
  a future lifecycle assumption, not present resource-delete authority.
- Proposed temporary restore PE/account lifetime is at most four hours, within the
  already adopted 80 access hours. The primary host remains at 730 hours.
- Proposed transfer, disk, exercise RU/operations and central DNS allocation are in
  the saved new meter question. Do not publish a full cost total until adopted/revised.
- No central Defender plans, policy workspaces, notification sub-meters, VPN, queue,
  Key Vault, production stamp or application-code deliverable is added. Inherited
  policy costs outside the project scope remain disclosed as unknown exposure.
